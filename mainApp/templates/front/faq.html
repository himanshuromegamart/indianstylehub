Home FAQ

What is NariFashionStore?

NariFashionStore is a clothing store offering a wide range of traditional, ethnic, and contemporary fashion for men, women, and kids. We bring the latest trends at affordable prices, delivered across India.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email or SMS. You can also log in to your account on NariFashionStore and view your order history to track the status of your order.

How do I find the right size?

We provide a detailed size chart on each product page. Please refer to the chart and measurements to choose the best fit. If you're still unsure, feel free to contact our customer care for guidance.

Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Yes, we accept returns and exchanges within 7 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in original packaging. Please visit our Return Policy page for complete details.

How do I care for my clothes?

We recommend following the wash care instructions mentioned on the label of each product. Most traditional and embroidered clothes should be dry cleaned or hand washed in cold water.

Do you restock sold-out items?

Some popular items are restocked regularly. You can click the "Notify Me" button on the product page to get an email when the item is back in stock.
